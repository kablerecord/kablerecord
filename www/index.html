Building businesses, raising builders.

I'm a multi-business founder teaching the next generation that character compounds faster than money. I started digging ditches at 10 years old in my father's concrete business. Today I run multiple companies across construction, technology, franchising, and coaching — generating millions in revenue while protecting what matters most: time with family.

After realizing my empire would die with me if I didn't teach my five kids to build — not just inherit — I wrote The Fourth Generation Formula, a book about breaking the 97% generational failure rate.
